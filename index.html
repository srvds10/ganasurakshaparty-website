About Us

Gana Suraksha Party (GSP) is a registered political party with its head office at Tamulpur, Assam. The party is led by its President, Naba Kumar Sarania, former Member of Parliament (Lok Sabha) from Kokrajhar. The party's election symbol is the torch.
